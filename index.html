Glycerol Trinitrate

GTN • Nitroglycerin (NTG)
Vasodilator (Organic Nitrate)

Bioactivated in vascular smooth muscle releasing Nitric Oxide (NO), which stimulates soluble guanylyl cyclase to increase cGMP. Dilates capacitance veins at low doses, reducing cardiac preload and wall tension; dilates arteries at higher doses.

Onset: 1–3 min (SL) t½: 2–8 min Bioavail: 10%–20% Tachyphylaxis: 8h window

Propofol

2,6-diisopropylphenol • 1% Lipid Emulsion
IV Anaesthetic / Sedative-Hypnotic

Potentiates GABAA receptors to produce rapid hypnosis. Rapid onset (<45s) and quick recovery driven by redistribution out of the vessel-rich brain. Causes preload/afterload reduction, respiratory depression, and transient apnea. Zero analgesic action.

Onset: < 30–45 sec Duration: 3–8 min Dist t½: 2–4 min Extra-hepatic: Lung
